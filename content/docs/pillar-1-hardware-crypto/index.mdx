# Pillar 1: Hardware, Cryptographic & Compute Verification

This pillar focuses on verifying who is training models, where the physical GPUs are located, and mathematically proving model execution without leaking proprietary weights or training data.

## Submodules in this Pillar

- **01. Chip Location Verification**: Latency triangulation and defense against VPN/relay spoofing.
- **02. Remote Attestation & TEEs**: Verifying model binaries inside secure enclaves.
- **03. Proof-of-Training & Cryptographic Logs**: Merkle trees of gradient updates and batch seeds.
- **04. Zero-Knowledge Proofs for ML (ZKML)**: Generating ZK-SNARK proofs of inference integrity. *(Claimed by Michael)*
- **05. Confidential Inference & MPC**: Multi-party computation and secret sharing for private queries.
- **06. Hardware Side-Channel Attacks**: GPU cache timing and memory trace analysis.

# Master Curriculum Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the complete set of 30 planned lab modules across all 6 technical pillars. 

Pillars 1 and 2 represent the active sprint. Pillars 3 through 6 represent upcoming modules open for research and development.

---

## Pillar 1: Hardware, Cryptographic & Compute Verification *(Active)*

*Focus: Verifying compute locations, TEE enclaves, and cryptographic proofs of training.*

1. **Chip Location Verification**: Delay-based latency triangulation and defense against VPN/relay spoofing.
2. **Remote Attestation & TEEs**: Cryptographic attestation quotes for model binaries and weights.
3. **Proof-of-Training & Cryptographic Logs**: Merkle trees of gradient updates and batch seeds.
4. **Zero-Knowledge Proofs for ML (ZKML)**: ZK-SNARK circuit compilation for private inference integrity. *(Claimed by Michael)*
5. **Confidential Inference & MPC**: Secret-sharing and homomorphic encryption wrappers.
6. **Hardware Side-Channel Attacks**: GPU memory access and cache timing reverse-engineering.

---

## Pillar 2: Infrastructure, Weight & Supply Chain Security *(Active)*

*Focus: Datacenter security, weight theft prevention, and supply chain integrity.*

7. **Weight Exfiltration & Covert Channels**: Steganographic weight smuggling via token logprobs. *(Claimed by Ashanti)*
8. **Egress Monitoring & Rate-Limiting**: Traffic inspection and entropy analysis for exfiltration defense.
9. **ML Supply Chain & Serialization Exploits**: Malicious PyTorch pickle exploits and safe safetensors migration.
10. **Model Extraction & Distillation Theft**: Query-efficient model cloning attacks and watermarking defenses.
11. **Inference Engine & KV-Cache Exploits**: Shared prefix caching side-channels in multi-tenant serving engines.

# AI Safety Research Lab (AISRL)

Welcome to the **AI Safety Research Lab** curriculum. This is a collection of hands-on, self-paced lab exercises designed to teach technical AI safety and security concepts through practical code, simulated environments, and automated grading.

## How the Curriculum is Structured

The curriculum is organized into six pillars:

- **Pillar 1: Hardware, Cryptographic & Compute Verification** *(Active)*
- **Pillar 2: Infrastructure, Weight & Supply Chain Security** *(Active)*
- **Pillar 3: Adversarial Robustness & Automated Red-Teaming** *(Roadmap)*
- **Pillar 4: Backdoors, Data Poisoning & Machine Unlearning** *(Roadmap)*
- **Pillar 5: Agentic Security, Sandboxing & AI Control** *(Roadmap)*
- **Pillar 6: Technical Governance, Auditing & Treaty Verification** *(Roadmap)*

## How Each Lab Works

Every lab in this course provides:
1. **A Technical Writeup**: Context on real-world threat models and defensive principles.
2. **Open-Source Code**: Starter code, simulated endpoints, or container environments.
3. **Self-Grading Tests**: Automated `pytest` suites allowing students to verify their solutions locally.
